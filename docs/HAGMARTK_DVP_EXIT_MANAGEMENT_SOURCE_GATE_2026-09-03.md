# HAGMARTK DVP — Exit Management Source Gate — 2026-09-03

## Source-backed statements

Fernando Pereira's public DIVAP material states that:

- entry is above the reversal pattern for buys and below it for sells;
- stop is below the reversal pattern for buys and above it for sells;
- Fibonacci extension is used to define trade targets;
- the source levels discussed are 61.8, 100, 161.8, 200 and 261.8;
- the public video describes the Fibonacci targets as zones where partial profit may be realized;
- the video explicitly acknowledges different management choices among traders, including taking partial profit at the first target or aiming farther.

## What the public source does NOT freeze

The inspected public material does not establish one mandatory universal rule for:

- which exact Fibonacci target closes 100% of the position;
- fixed percentages to realize at each target;
- moving the stop after a partial target;
- time-based exit after N candles;
- forced close at 20 candles.
## Frozen research decision

1. The 20-bar window remains telemetry only. It is not a DIVAP exit rule.
2. EXIT_2R remains a HAGMARTK quantitative benchmark, not an original DIVAP claim.
3. Fibonacci partial management remains fidelity research until a deterministic source-backed allocation/exit policy is established.
4. Unresolved positions at an arbitrary telemetry horizon must remain open/censored, never silently converted to wins, losses or breakeven.
5. Any future automated exit policy must be separately versioned and labeled as source-backed or HAGMARTK-authored.

## Evidence boundary

Marketing pages currently publish historical accuracy and average risk/reward claims for DIVAP-related products. Those claims are contextual external claims, not accepted as HAGMARTK validation evidence and not used to tune this gate.

The present purpose is methodological fidelity, not performance replication.
